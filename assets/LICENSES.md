# Asset licences (PLAN_V6 milestone 1)

All furniture models used by v6 are public-domain dedicated (CC0 1.0). Checked on 2026-11-15.
They are not committed (git-ignored `data/assets_raw/`); `python scripts/download_assets_v6.py` fetches them.

## Kenney Furniture Kit 2.0
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit (Kenney, www.kenney.nl), 140 models.
- Licence: Creative Commons Zero, CC0 (stated on the page and in the kit's `License.txt`).
- Used: bedDouble, televisionModern, pottedPlant, lampRoundFloor, kitchenCabinet, kitchenStove, kitchenSink,
  kitchenFridgeLarge, kitchenBar, bathtub, toilet, bathroomCabinetDrawer, cabinetTelevision, rugRectangle.
